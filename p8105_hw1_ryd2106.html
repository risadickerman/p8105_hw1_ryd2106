Problem 1

Th penguins data set from the palmerpenguins package describes morphological features of 344 different penguins. 8 different variables were observed. These included the bird’s bill_length_mm, bill_depth_mm, flipper_length_mm, body_mass_g, sex in addition to the species of the bird, the island they lived on, and the year the samples were collected.

This study found several interesting statistics of the penguins on the following islands: Torgersen, Biscoe, Dream. For example, it was found that the mean flipper length of all these birds lie at 201mm. The flipper length of the Adelie penguin had a mean of 190mm, the Chinstrap species mean was 196mm, and the Gentoo’s mean was 217mm.
